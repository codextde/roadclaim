Automatic recording

You never press start.

Roadclaim notices when you drive and records on its own, then stops when you park. Your phone stays in your pocket or in the mount, and the drive is waiting for you when you arrive.

  • Motion activity and location wake the recorder when a drive begins
  • Stops by itself after a few minutes parked
  • Short hops under 500 m are dropped
  • Manual start and stop if you prefer

Stats nobody else counts

Your drive, in numbers.

Every drive is measured on your phone: how often you braked, every left and right turn, lane changes on the highway, the climbs and descents, stops and the hardest acceleration.

  • Distance, moving time, stopped time and stops
  • Elevation gain, highest point, steepest grade
  • Peak g-force and hardest acceleration
  • Lifetime totals, season stats and a monthly recap

Relive it

Share cards and Ride Replay.

Every drive ends with a card worth posting. Then watch the whole route again in about thirty seconds, flying behind your car over 3D satellite imagery.

Share cards

Route sketch, distance, time, place and date. Switch on details for brake presses, turns, stops, lane changes and elevation. Square and story formats.

Ride Replay

A chase camera follows your route across 3D satellite maps. Scrub anywhere, change the speed, watch distance and time tick up.

Trophies

Bronze, silver and gold for distance, streaks, night drives, early starts, climbs, countries and cells held.

Territory

The world is a hexagon grid. Take it.

The map is split into hexagons about 300 m across. Every drive claims each one you pass through. Empty cells become yours, cells held by someone else are stolen, your own are refreshed.

  • See everything you have ever held and what you hold now
  • Monthly seasons with boards for most held, top stealers and top gainers
  • Clubs pool their turf on a shared club map

Privacy by design

Your routes never leave your phone.

Drives are stored on your device. To play territory and leaderboards, the app sends drive totals and the ids of the hexagons you passed, never GPS coordinates.

No sign-up

An anonymous account is created on first launch. No email, no phone number, no password. A recovery key moves your account to a new phone.

Cells, not coordinates

The server knows which hexagons you crossed, about 300 m wide, never your exact route, home or speed trace.

Your choice

Keep everything on this device with uploads off, hide from public leaderboards, or switch on cloud backup of full drives. Backup is off by default.

No ads, no tracking

No advertising, no analytics, no tracking SDKs. Delete your account in the app or on this website at any time.

Safety first

No stat is worth your safety.

Roadclaim needs no interaction while you drive. Never touch your phone at the wheel, and follow the speed limit. There is no speed leaderboard, on purpose.

Open source

Read the code. Run your own server.
