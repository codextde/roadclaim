Delete your account

You can delete your Roadclaim account at any time. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

In the app

Open Roadclaim, go to Profile, then Settings, and tap Delete account.

Without the app

No longer have the app or your phone? Enter your recovery key below. You find it in the app under Profile, Settings, Recovery key.

What is deleted

Your profile, drive summaries, cells, friendships, club ownership, trophies, AI car mods and cloud backups are removed from our server. Drives stored on your phone stay there until you delete the app. Details in the privacy policy.

Lost your recovery key? Email kontakt@codext.de with your username and we will help.
